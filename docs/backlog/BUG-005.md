# BUG-005: New expense does not appear until I refresh the page

**Reported by:** Hoa (Finance)
**Severity:** Medium
**Environment:** Chrome 130 and Safari 18, macOS 15, app at http://localhost:3000

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open the app.
2. Fill the **Add expense** form: date `2026-03-20`, amount `5.5`, category `food`, note `Tea`.
3. Click **Add**.

## Expected

The form clears and the new "Tea" row shows at the top of the list right away.
The pager count goes from 12 to 13 and the March summary updates.

## Actual

The form clears, but the list and the summary do not change. Nothing happens until I press F5,
then the new row is there. The expense *is* saved, it just does not show.

## Notes

Deleting a row updates the list immediately, so I expected adding to behave the same.
I thought my click did not register and added the same expense three times before I noticed.
