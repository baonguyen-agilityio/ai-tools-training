# BUG-004: Expense dated the 1st of a month is counted in the previous month

**Reported by:** Sarah (remote teammate, New York)
**Severity:** High
**Environment:** Chrome 130, macOS 15, app started on my laptop (system timezone America/New_York), seeded data

## Steps to reproduce

1. Start the app on a machine whose timezone is America/New_York (or any US timezone).
2. Open the app and pick `2026-01` in **Monthly summary**.
3. Compare with the list: the only January expenses are dated 01-03, 01-10, 01-15, 01-28.

## Expected

January total is `186.25` (12.5 + 45 + 8.75 + 120).

## Actual

January total is `216.35`, and `food` shows `51.35` instead of `21.25`.
The extra `30.1` is the "Groceries" expense dated **2026-02-01**. Picking `2026-02` confirms it:
February is missing that expense.

## Notes

My colleagues in Vietnam see the correct numbers on the same data, so this looks tied to where
the app runs. Any expense entered on the 1st of a month lands in the wrong month for me.
Reproducible every time; happy to screen-share.
