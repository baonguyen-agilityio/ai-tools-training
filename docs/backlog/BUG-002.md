# BUG-002: Monthly total shows a long string of decimals

**Reported by:** Minh (Ops)
**Severity:** Medium
**Environment:** Chrome 130, macOS 15, app at http://localhost:3000, seeded data

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open the app.
2. In **Monthly summary**, pick `2026-02`.

## Expected

Total reads `315.29`. Per-category lines also have at most 2 decimals.

## Actual

Total reads `315.28999999999996`. The `food` line reads `40.09` but I have seen other
months/categories with the same kind of garbage after adding a few expenses with cents.

## Notes

January (`2026-01`) looks fine: `186.25`. It seems to depend on which amounts are in the month.
Screenshot attached in the thread. This goes into a report people actually read, so it needs
to stop happening, not just be rounded on my screen.
