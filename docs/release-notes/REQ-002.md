# Release notes: Monthly budget with warning

For Linh (Finance) · October 2026

## What you can do now

- **Set a budget for each month.** Enter one overall amount for the month.
- **Add caps for food, transport, bills and fun.** Caps are optional. A category without a cap still counts toward the overall budget.
- **See the colour change before the money runs out.** A line turns amber when 80 % of its budget is spent and red at 100 %. Below 80 % there is no colour.
- **Read what is left.** Each budgeted line shows "Left: 50.00", or "Over by 35.50" once it has gone over.
- **Get a short note right after adding an expense.** If the expense brings its category or the month's total near or over budget, one line appears under the Add button, for example "food: over budget (Over by 35.50)" or "total: 88% of budget (Left: 179.50)". Nothing appears while everything is fine. Adding an expense is never blocked.
- **Start each month from the last budget.** A month with no saved budget uses the most recent one, marked "copied from 2026-09, not saved". It becomes that month's own budget once you press Save.
- **Try it on October 2026 demo data.** Food sits at 90 % of its cap and the month's total at 88 %, so both show amber.

## How to use it

1. In **Monthly summary**, choose the **Month**.
2. Enter the **Budget total**, and any caps you want for **food**, **transport**, **bills** or **fun**.
3. Press **Save**. You will see "Saved for 2026-10." (with your month).
4. Add expenses as usual with **Add**. Check the colours and "Left" or "Over by" in Monthly summary, and the note under the Add button.

## Fixed before release

Our testers found three issues before release. All are fixed:

- **Switching months while a budget was saving.** The screen could show the budget and totals of the wrong month. It now always shows the month you picked.
- **The note after adding an expense stayed after a delete.** It now disappears when you delete an expense, so it never describes spending that has changed.
- **Clearing the Month field.** The previous month's totals, budget and export link stayed on screen. Now the summary and budget empty out until you pick a month.

## Not included

These were decided with you while scoping this release:

- **No sign-in.** Anyone with the page can set or change a budget. Who may change budgets will be handled with user accounts in a later release.
- **No deleting a saved month's budget.** To change it, edit the numbers and press Save.
- **No "on pace to overspend" forecast.** Colours are based on what has been spent so far.
- **Budgets are not in the CSV export.** The export lists expenses only, as before.
- **No email, chat or phone alerts.** Warnings show in the page only.
